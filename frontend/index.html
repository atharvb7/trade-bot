      Questions

      What people ask first.

      Does Trade-bot trade real money?

      No. It runs in paper mode by default. Live trading needs both TRADING_MODE=live and a confirmation file, and the plan only allows it after positive results on unseen data and four weeks of paper trading.

      Why are most signals rejected?

      Costs. On 5-minute bars the stop is about 0.2% of price, so fees and slippage come close to the whole amount at risk. The minimum edge rule blocks any trade whose target is not more than three times the estimated cost, and most signals stop there.

      What does R mean?

      R is the amount at risk on a trade: the distance from entry to stop, times the number of shares. A result of −1 R means the trade lost what it risked. A result of +1.5 R means it gained one and a half times that.

      Which broker and data does it use?

      Historical candles come from Fyers. Orders are planned through Zerodha Kite Connect, inside one module that a test keeps separate from the rest of the code.

      Is any of this investment advice?

      No. Trade-bot is a personal research project, and its results so far are losses after costs. The terms explain this in full.
