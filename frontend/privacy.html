Privacy policy

Effective

In short

  • This site has no accounts, sign-up forms, comments or payments.
  • It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Every file, including the fonts, is served from this site. Your browser does not contact any other company when you read it.
  • The only personal data involved is what any web server receives to deliver a page, and anything you choose to send us by email.

Who runs this site

This website, , is run by ("we", "us"). You can contact us about this policy at .

Data your browser sends when you visit

To deliver a page, your browser sends the server a standard request. It includes your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, your browser and operating system version, and the address of the page that linked you here, if any.

The site is hosted by . The hosting provider may keep these requests in server logs to run the service and protect it from abuse. We do not use these logs to identify you, build a profile of you or combine them with other data. How long the logs are kept is set by the hosting provider's own privacy policy.

Settings stored on your device

If you choose the Light or Dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name tradebot-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Choosing Auto removes it, and you can also clear it through your browser's site data settings.

Nothing else is stored on your device. The site does not use cookies.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use them only to reply, and we keep the conversation for as long as it is needed for that purpose. We do not add you to any mailing list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not collect financial details, trading account details or broker credentials. This site never asks for them.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The site is not meant for children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation may give you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it erased. Because this site collects almost nothing, most requests will be about emails you have sent us. Write to and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting anything new, such as analytics or a contact form, we will update this page before that change goes live and change the effective date at the top.
